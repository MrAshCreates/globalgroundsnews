Social media has become our source to gain knowledge on the news and topics of the world today. Often it is biased and difficult to interact with it and spread your knowledgeable opinions. But what other options do you have? For a lot of the population we seek Articles and news channels to broadcast what is going on and share similar views as you. They all tend to be biased to an extent and that’s how they hold an audience if you constantly consume opinions that are similar to yours then you are more likely to stay around and listen. We like when we can interact and share our views with people that share the same opinion. But we often dislike opinions that differ from our own. So what can we do about it how can we get better news outlets and sources that can educate the people and allow the people to share.  

This is where global ground news comes in. We will be the voice of the people in a way unseen by current social media platforms. Think of it as a global group chat where you can chat back and forth with the fellow people of the world you can go to conversations where the people share the same views as you or you can have one on one talks with those who differ. Global grounds is built to change the way we think. To open the world to change and knowledge and to help us leap forward into a more positive and prosperous world. We don’t claim to change how you think or claim to make a difference.  We are the people. We have to come together to communicate and fight for the change. 

Global grounds will create active conversations and communities for people to connect and network with one another. But they expire. Once a conversation ends it will have an article created about the results of the opinions as well as information that is equally biased. From that we plan on creating podcasts and videos to educate those that seek short form content. 

The site will contain many features for free and a subscription for some bonus features. The site will be built off donations to keep it running. We might even make a conversation for how the site should be built and how things should work. This project although we would love to be open source can’t completely be. We are focused on the security and safety of others as much are we want a trust system in place it just can’t always work. We can’t have people getting doxxed and get put into dangerous situations because of their opinions and views this is why by default all accounts will be anonymous unless two accounts are added by one another and agree to both share their identity.
